# RackTrail logo concepts

Three exploratory logo boards, each with a wordmark, app-icon concept and monochrome treatment. These are design options; the application icon has not been changed.

## Prompts

### 01-rack-and-route

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: RackTrail logo concept presentation, option 1 named Rack & Route. Design a sophisticated flat vector-style identity for RackTrail, a native desktop application for designing network maps. Main symbol: a compact abstract three-unit server rack with a single elegant cable route weaving down the rack and ending in a clear circular network node. Make the shape simple, distinctive, confident, scalable to a Windows taskbar icon at 24 pixels. Deep navy, turquoise and a tiny warm gold accent, restrained two-color mark. Exact wordmark text: RackTrail (capital R and T, spelled R-a-c-k-T-r-a-i-l), clean custom geometric sans-serif; no slogan. A polished landscape brand board on clean warm white: large full-color symbol and RackTrail wordmark horizontally across the upper half; lower half contains a matching rounded-square desktop icon on navy and a monochrome black symbol on white, all the SAME consistent identity. Plenty of whitespace. No photography, gradients, 3D, mockups, watermark, extra words or existing vendor logos.

### 02-rt-monogram

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: RackTrail logo concept presentation, option 2, Route Monogram. Create a bold and polished flat vector-style logo for RackTrail, a desktop network mapping editor. Main symbol: an original clever RT monogram constructed from a single continuous right-angle network route, with two subtle square or round endpoints; negative space clearly implies R and T without feeling like a generic circuit board. Simple enough for a tiny Windows desktop icon. Strong indigo/navy and vivid electric blue with white negative space; choose two restrained colors. Exact wordmark RackTrail (capital R and T, spelled R-a-c-k-T-r-a-i-l), precise modern sans-serif, slightly softer than the angular mark, no tagline. Landscape professional logo board on white with the main mark plus wordmark in the upper half and in the lower half a matching rounded-square app icon and black-only logo specimen, using exactly the same mark. Generous whitespace, crisp contours, high design craft. No gradients, shadows, 3D, photography, watermark, decorative backgrounds, additional copy, or existing company logos.

### 03-topology-trail

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: RackTrail logo concept presentation, option 3, Topology Trail. Design a distinctive premium flat vector-style brand identity for RackTrail, a local desktop network-map designer. Main symbol: three or four small connected network nodes and a clean ascending angular path, compactly composed into a subtly trail-like or map-like emblem that feels navigable and organized rather than a generic globe or compass. Architectural precision, approachable, simple thick strokes that remain clear at 24px. Use dark graphite and a distinctive warm amber/orange accent with white; no turquoise or blue in this option. Exact wordmark: RackTrail (capital R and T, spelled R-a-c-k-T-r-a-i-l), sturdy elegant sans-serif with balanced spacing, no slogan. A landscape white identity presentation showing a large colored emblem with RackTrail wordmark in the upper half, and matching dark rounded-square app icon plus one-color black symbol in the lower half. Keep every specimen the same identity. Ample whitespace, clean forms. No photo, gradients, 3D, drop shadows, watermark, extra words, or existing vendor logos.
